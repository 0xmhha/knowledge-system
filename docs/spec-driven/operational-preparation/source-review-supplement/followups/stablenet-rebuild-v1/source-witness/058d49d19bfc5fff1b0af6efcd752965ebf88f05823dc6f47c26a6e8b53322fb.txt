# Native coin issuance and burn via NativeCoinManager precompile

**Status:** verified · **Subsystem:** A5 (Native Coin & Account Extra) · **Type:** B3

Native coin mint, burn, and transfer are precompile operations on
NativeCoinManager. coinManagerMint credits a target address;
coinManagerBurn debits an account; coinManagerTransfer moves between
accounts without changing total supply. All three gate on
canRunCoinManger, which requires the caller to be NativeCoinAdapter
(0x1000) and the opcode to be CALL (not DELEGATECALL or STATICCALL).
Total-supply changes therefore route exclusively through 0x1000.

## Invariants
- coinManagerMint and coinManagerBurn require the EVM caller to be NativeCoinAdapter (0x1000); any other caller fails canRunCoinManger.
- DELEGATECALL and STATICCALL into the precompile are rejected; only CALL is allowed.

## Pitfalls
- Assuming mint and burn can be invoked by any contract: they are gated to NativeCoinAdapter, so direct ERC-20-style external calls do not move native supply.
